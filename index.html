🪂Tandem skydive · SkyKefCall first

Sde Teiman (~10 min N of Be’er Sheva) · ~1h20 drive · ages 10+ · max 110 kg (extra charge over 90 kg / 1.90 m)

Real jump from 10,000 or 12,000 ft with an instructor. Site prices: Tandem 10 ₪1,050, Tandem 12 ₪1,250, video+photos +₪250. Plan on ~3 hours there. Site lists Sun–Thu 9:00–19:00, but its FAQ says jumps happen “Fri, Sat & holidays”, so call to confirm they jump today.

📞 1700-705-867 Book online Prices

🛩️Powered parachute (“flying ATV”) · Israel ExtremeCall first

Ein Vered airstrip, Sharon · ~1h10 drive · ages 6+ · pilot + 1 passenger

20 min flight over the Sharon fields to the coast: ₪500 (₪650 with photos); 40 min with photos ₪1,050. They fly in the early morning or ~2 hours before sunset, so today’s realistic option is the evening slot. Book by phone.

📞 053-331-0639 📞 072-394-0984 Flights & prices

🏜️RZR / Tomcar desert tour · Canaan (Kfar Adumim)Call first

Kfar Adumim junction, Judean Desert · ~25 min drive · drivers 18+ with a valid licence · riders 16+ (no licence needed)

Guided off-road loops to desert lookouts (Wadi Qelt / St. George area). Tours run 1–4 hours. Weekend.co.il lists ₪700 for 1.5 h and ₪800 for 2 h per 4-seat vehicle (that listing may be old). Pre-booking only.

📞 02-535-5351 Website
